Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026
🔒 This app collects no personal data.

Korean Fidget (“the App”) is designed to help you learn to read and build Korean (Hangul) syllables. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains, in plain language, what the App does and does not do with your information.

1. We do not collect any personal data

Korean Fidget does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The App has no sign-up, no login, no account, and no analytics or tracking. We do not know who you are, and we never see what you type or build in the App.

2. The App works fully offline — no servers, no API

Everything the App does happens entirely on your device. Korean Fidget does not send any data to us or to any third party, and it does not require an internet connection to function.

In particular, translations are not fetched from any online service or API. Instead, the App includes a built-in dictionary of commonly used Korean words and expressions that is packaged directly inside the App. When you build a word, the meaning is looked up locally from this bundled list — nothing leaves your device.

3. Free dictionary updates

Because the dictionary is built in rather than pulled from a server, we expand it over time. As the bundled word and expression list grows, these additions are delivered as free app updates at no extra cost to you. You never pay more to receive a larger dictionary.

4. Pronunciation (text-to-speech)

When you tap the speaker button to hear a word, the App uses your device’s own built-in text-to-speech voices (for example, Apple’s Korean voices on iPhone). This processing happens on your device. No audio or text is sent to us. For the most natural pronunciation, you may optionally download a Premium Korean voice in your device’s accessibility settings.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected at all, no personal information from children is ever gathered or stored.

6. Third-party services

The App does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or data-collection SDKs. The App is distributed through the Apple App Store, and your use of the App Store is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
